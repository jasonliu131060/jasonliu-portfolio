About.

I’m most interested in where continuous-time hardware meets digital control.

I’m an electrical engineering student at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering. I enjoy systems that combine analog sensing, power conversion, embedded firmware, and feedback, especially when the design has to survive noise, timing limits, efficiency targets, and safety requirements. My work so far includes an isolated vehicle power converter, a dual-camera edge-AI wearable, vehicle control electronics, and an autonomous robot.
